Delete Your SipSpot Account

Last updated: August 2026

You can delete your SipSpot account and all of its data yourself, from inside the app, at any time. It takes about fifteen seconds, and it is permanent — there is no deactivated or hidden state that we could restore later.

You do not need to email us, call us, or wait for approval. Deletion is completed entirely in the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open SipSpot and sign in, if you aren't already.
  2. Tap the Profile tab (bottom right).
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete account.

You'll be signed out as soon as it's done, and the account no longer exists.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (Apple, Google, or Facebook)
  • Your username — it's released, and anyone can claim it afterwards
  • Your profile photo and bio
  • Every spot you've saved
  • Your friends, and any pending friend requests in either direction

What we keep

Problem reports and spot suggestions you sent us stay in our moderation queue, because they're about a bar or restaurant rather than about you — deleting them would leave wrong happy hour times in the app for everyone else. They are anonymised: the link between the report and your account is erased with the account, and nothing identifying you remains attached to it.

If you subscribe to SipSpot Pro

SipSpot Pro is billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel the subscription first, then delete your account:

  • iPhone / iPad — Manage subscriptions in the App Store
  • Android — Manage subscriptions in Google Play

If you can't get into the app

Lost the device, or can't sign in? Email support@sipspot.app from the address on the account and we'll delete it for you. This is only a fallback for people who can't reach the in-app option — if you can open SipSpot, use the steps above and you'll be done before we'd read the email.
